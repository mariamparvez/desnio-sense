# Customer Razorpay checkout

Customer payments use the saved Firebase product price, active product offer and an eligible server-validated coupon. The browser never supplies the amount to charge. Live checkout requires verified email, saved delivery details, real product options/colours and an explicit available stock count. Custom artwork and unlisted phone-model requests cannot enter this payment flow.

Production-only protected variables: `RAZORPAY_LIVE_KEY_ID`, Sensitive `RAZORPAY_LIVE_KEY_SECRET`, Sensitive `RAZORPAY_LIVE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Set `RAZORPAY_LIVE_ENABLED=true` only after registering the Live Mode webhook and verifying deployment checks. Sandbox variables remain separate. No credentials belong in source, logs or browser code. `/api/payment-readiness` verifies provider authentication and a Firestore read/write without creating a payment.

Register `https://www.desniosense.com/api/razorpay-webhook` with the matching live signing secret and events `payment.captured`, `order.paid`, `payment.failed`, `refund.processed`, `refund.failed`. The receiver validates the exact raw bytes and selects the signing configuration that matches; sandbox notifications cannot confirm a live order.

Checkout atomically saves the order, locks its price and reserves stock before creating one Razorpay order. Its idempotency key is customer-bound and bound to cart, coupon and delivery details. Uncertain provider responses stay locked for support review rather than creating another payment order. Unpaid reservations expire after 20 minutes and are reclaimed during subsequent checkout activity after checking the provider first. There is no scheduled cleanup job; without new checkouts an abandoned reservation can remain held until checkout resumes or the order is cancelled.

Callback signature verification and provider-side amount/currency/order/capture checks precede the Paid state. A matching authorised live payment is captured server-side while the reservation is active. Browser redirects alone cannot mark an order paid. Duplicate notifications and older failures cannot reverse a confirmed capture. Confirmation and cart clearing occur only after verified Paid status. Disabling new payments leaves reconciliation available for existing attempts.

Cancellation before production restores reserved stock once. Captured money for a cancelled checkout is labelled Refund pending and cannot enter fulfilment. Refunds must be reviewed and initiated by the owner in Razorpay; this release does not automatically issue refunds. Refund webhooks/status checks update the recorded state from the provider, including partial refunds. Do not ship Pending, Authorized, Refund pending, Partially refunded or Refunded orders. Shipping charges remain zero as in the existing store; do not enable customer sales until delivery coverage, final pricing, options and stock have been confirmed by the owner.

Validate the server test suite and complete site checks/build, then verify actual provider authentication and Firestore read/write. A real paid/refunded transaction requires the owner to perform or explicitly authorise that payment; automated fixture success is not a completed live transaction.
