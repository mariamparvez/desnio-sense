# Desnio Sense search launch

Canonical website: https://www.desniosense.com/ . Brand spelling: Desnio Sense; alternate spelling: DesnioSense. Existing Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links belong to the organization identity. Keep the same spelling and website link on the owner's actual profiles. Do not create artificial reviews, backlinks, addresses or duplicate business listings.

## Website changes

The home page declares one connected WebSite, Organization and WebPage graph. Server-rendered collection pages contain ordinary links to populated categories and audiences, original collection descriptions and canonical URLs. Product descriptions stay on product detail pages. Equivalent type/audience URL combinations normalize to one canonical destination. Internal searches and empty filtered collections are noindex; customer, checkout and admin pages are noindex. Product pages retain truthful Product metadata without invented reviews or purchase availability.

https://www.desniosense.com/sitemap.xml is generated from published Firebase products and includes populated category URLs, product images and valid product update timestamps. Admin changes reach the public catalogue and subsequent sitemap responses; the public catalogue has a short cache. A sitemap supports discovery, not a promise of indexing or ranking. No new payments or pricing changes are part of this SEO release.

## Required owner step: Search Console

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/welcome and sign in to the owner's Google account.
2. Choose URL prefix and enter exactly https://www.desniosense.com/ .
3. Expand HTML tag and provide that public google-site-verification tag to the website maintainer. Never provide a Google password or OTP.
4. The maintainer adds the exact token to the home page head, deploys it and confirms it is present in public HTML. Then click Verify. Keep the tag permanently.
5. In Sitemaps, submit https://www.desniosense.com/sitemap.xml . Inspect the canonical home page, shop page and main product categories with URL Inspection. Use Test live URL and Request indexing when available.
6. Inspect Page indexing, Crawl stats and any Manual actions/Security issues. Record Google's actual reported status, not an inference from a general web search. A domain-wide property can also be added later with the owner's GoDaddy DNS TXT verification; preserve mail records.

Registration, ownership verification and sitemap submission have not been completed unless Google confirms them. No Search Console access token is present in source.

## Follow-up work

After verification, use Search Console to establish a baseline for the queries Desnio Sense and DesnioSense, indexed product URLs, impressions and clicks. Review weekly rather than repeatedly requesting indexing for unchanged URLs. Use the owner's real social channels to announce the store with its canonical URL. Obtain genuine mentions from customers, collaborators and relevant publications; do not buy links or manufacture brand searches.

When actual prices, stock, product options and customer payments are ready, add valid purchasable Offer metadata and consider Google Merchant Center product listings. Ensure displayed price, discounts, shipping and returns match the actual checkout. Do not advertise placeholder prices or unconfirmed inventory through product feeds. Create useful category copy and original product detail content around the actual merchandise, then evaluate real query data before adding more pages. Do not duplicate thin keyword/location pages.

Google chooses results, spelling corrections and site names. The website cannot remove unrelated legitimate results, guarantee first position or guarantee that only Desnio Sense appears. Re-crawling and processing can take days or weeks.
