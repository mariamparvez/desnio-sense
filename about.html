About Desnio Sense

Hi, I’m Mariam.

Founder of Desnio Sense. Boy mom. Always thinking of something to create.

I’ve always wanted to make graphics feel approachable. I want everyone to find something they connect with, something that feels like them.

Desnio Sense has been in my mind since 2019. It’s a passion project I’ve held onto for years, and I’m finally making it happen.

I want the things we wear, carry and use every day to have a little of our own personality in them. That’s what I’m here to make.

The other part of me

Being a mom is a creative job, too.

I’m a boy mom, and honestly, motherhood asks me to be creative every single day. Every little thing I do for my son becomes something I want to put my own imagination into.

That same care follows me into Desnio Sense. The thought I put into things for him, the excitement of making something special, the urge to try an idea just to see it come to life. It all comes from the same place.

He’s my biggest reason to keep going. In sha Allah, one day he’ll look at what I’ve built and see a mom who kept making time for something she believed in.

Desnio Sense is my baby, too.

I’m giving it the same love, patience and creativity I bring to being a mom. I’m learning as I go, but I know how much this means to me.

Thank you for being here. It means a lot to finally share it with you.

Mariam Parvez
Founder, Desnio Sense
